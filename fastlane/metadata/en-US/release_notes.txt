A new tab for developers, low-disk alerts, and easier explanations.

• Dev Projects tab — Tell Sweep where you keep your code and it lists folders that come back when deleted, like node_modules, .venv and Pods, biggest first.
• Low disk alerts — Get a menu bar alert when free space drops below the level you choose. Turn it on or off in Settings.
• Easier explanations — Descriptions in the cleanup list, CPU tab and confirmation windows were rewritten in plain words, and safety labels are clearer.
• New icons — The CPU, Space View and Dev Projects icons now show what each one does.
• Fixes — Restoring files now brings them back in other tabs too, and large and duplicate files can be restored.
