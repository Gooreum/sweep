Sweep now looks further and tells you more.

• Trash — See what's in the Trash by size and empty only what you choose, including iCloud Drive and external drive trash.
• Wider search — Find large and duplicate files across your home folder and folders you add, plus iOS device backups, Mail attachments and Gradle, Cargo and Maven caches.
• Disk Map verdicts — Every item is marked Safe to delete, Needs review or Protected, with the reason.
• Sortable lists — Click a column header to sort by name, size, date or safety.
• Live free space — Available space updates right after you clean up or undo.
• Fixes — Files deleted from another tab no longer reappear in results, and more.
