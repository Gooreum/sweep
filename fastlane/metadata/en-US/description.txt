[Your code grows. Your free space shrinks.]

Xcode build artifacts. Old simulator files. Forgotten downloads. Duplicate files.

Over time, development leaves behind gigabytes of files you may no longer need.

Meet Sweep — a disk cleanup utility built for Mac developers.

[Find the clutter. Know what's safe. Clean with confidence.]

■ BUILT FOR DEVELOPER MACHINES

Find space-consuming files across your development environment with a single scan using Search All.

• Xcode DerivedData and build caches
• iOS simulator caches and device support files
• npm, Yarn, pnpm, and Expo caches
• Swift Package Manager, CocoaPods, and Homebrew caches
• Large files in Downloads
• Duplicate files with identical content

Results are grouped into Files to Clean, Large Files, and Duplicates, so you can see how much space you could reclaim before removing anything.

■ NOT EVERYTHING SHOULD BE DELETED

Some files can be regenerated in seconds. Others may be difficult or impossible to recover.

Sweep classifies cleanup items into three safety levels:

• SAFE — Files that can be regenerated
• REVIEW — Files that require your attention
• DANGER — Files that may be impossible to recover

Only safe items are preselected.

Important development files, including Xcode Archives, are protected from accidental cleanup.

■ LARGE FILES: FIND WHAT YOU FORGOT

That old DMG. A downloaded video. An archive you opened once.

Sweep finds files larger than 100 MB in your Downloads folder and sorts them by size.

Review when files were downloaded and decide what stays.

■ DUPLICATES: KEEP ONE COPY

Different names don't always mean different files.

Sweep compares file contents to identify exact duplicates, excluding the earliest downloaded copy from cleanup candidates.

■ SEE WHERE YOUR SPACE GOES

Explore your storage with Space View.

Compare file and folder sizes, navigate directories, and quickly show items in Finder, copy locations, or move files to the Trash.

■ FIND OUT WHY YOUR MAC IS BUSY

The CPU tab lists the programs keeping your Mac busy, from busiest to quietest, using the same numbers as Activity Monitor.

Click a program to see what it is, who started it, how long it has been running, its memory use, the command it was started with, and where it lives — in plain words. Sweep also tells you which built-in macOS programs you should leave running.

Because of App Store sandbox rules, some programs started by macOS itself cannot be listed one by one and appear as a single combined row.

■ CLEAN WITHOUT SECOND-GUESSING

Sweep is designed to make cleanup deliberate and reversible.

• Files are moved to the macOS Trash, not permanently erased
• Restore your most recent cleanup
• Review safety classifications before deleting
• Keep cleanup operations within defined locations
• See how much space will be freed after emptying the Trash

You're always in control of what gets removed.

■ OLD-SCHOOL LOOK. MODERN MAC UTILITY.

Inspired by classic 1990s desktop software.

Pixel-style icons, beveled buttons, gray panels, and compact file lists bring back the charm of early desktop computing.

Retro on the outside. Practical on the inside.

■ STAY IN YOUR FLOW

• Check disk usage from the menu bar
• Start scans without opening the main window
• Press ⌘R to scan
• Press ⌘⌫ to clean
• Resize table columns to fit your workflow

■ YOUR FILES STAY YOURS

Sweep works entirely offline.

No accounts. No cloud uploads. No analytics. No data collection.

The Mac App Store version uses macOS sandbox permissions. You choose which supported folders Sweep can access, and you can revoke that access at any time.
