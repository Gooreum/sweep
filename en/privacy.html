Sweep Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026 · Updated October 14, 2026

Sweep does not collect any personal information. It never connects to the internet, and file lists and contents are processed only on your Mac.

1. Information we collect

Sweep does not collect or store any personal information, including your name, email address, device identifiers, location or usage history. There is no account or sign-in.

2. File access

To find files to clean up, Sweep reads the names, sizes and dates of files in your Downloads folder and in the folders you explicitly allow (such as ~/Library, ~/.npm, ~/.expo or Applications). To detect duplicates, it compares file contents.

  • All of this happens on your Mac. Results are never sent anywhere.
  • Only the files you choose to clean up are moved to the Trash, and you can restore them from there.
  • A short local record of each cleanup (item counts, sizes and dates) is kept on your Mac to show how much space you have freed. It contains no file contents and never leaves your Mac.
  • Folder access permissions are stored only on this Mac and are removed when you delete the app. You can also revoke them at any time in Sweep.

3. Network

Sweep does not connect to the network. It contains no SDKs from third-party services such as advertising, analytics or crash reporting.

4. Sharing with third parties

Because Sweep collects no information, there is nothing to share with or entrust to third parties.

5. Children's privacy

Sweep does not collect personal information from users of any age.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the changes and the new effective date will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, please email mg.seo.dev@gmail.com.
